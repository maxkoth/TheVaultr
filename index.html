The problem

Got a box of cards and no idea what they're worth? You're not alone.

Most collections are a mystery. Boxes and piles of cards, a few that might be valuable, and no clear way to tell which is which. Going through them one at a time takes forever, pricing guides are confusing, and one wrong move means selling a gem for pocket change — or grading a card that isn't worth the fee.

Which cards matter?

It's nearly impossible to know which cards in a pile are valuable and which are just bulk.

What's it actually worth?

Prices swing constantly. Without live sold data, you're guessing in the dark.

Sell, grade, or hold?

Even when you know the value, the right next move is rarely obvious.
